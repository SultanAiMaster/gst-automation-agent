What is Bharat GST AI?

Bharat GST AI is designed to simplify GST compliance for businesses in India through automation and AI assistance.

The goal is to reduce the complexity of GST return filing by providing businesses with a simple digital system that manages reminders, data organization, and return preparation.

Currently, businesses spend hours calculating taxes, managing invoices, and ensuring compliance. Our platform automates all of this - from GST calculation to GSTR report generation, from deadline reminders to AI-powered tax advice.

Core Concept

  • Automated GST Calculation

    Instant CGST, SGST, IGST based on transaction type

  • GSTR Report Generation

    One-click GSTR-1, GSTR-3B, GSTR-9 reports

  • ITC Tracking & Optimization

    Maximize Input Tax Credit savings

  • AI-Powered GST Advice

    24/7 AI assistance for complex tax queries

  • Filing Deadline Reminders

    Never miss a deadline with smart notifications
